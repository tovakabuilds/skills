# Pick Angle

Goal, then emotion, then angle. One post, one job.

## When to use
After a format is picked and before hooks are written. The factory runs it at step 4.

## What you'll need
The brief (audience, voice, offers, never_say, facts), the chosen format, and topics. If there are no topics, propose them from the brief: questions the audience asks, mistakes they make, results the business delivers, behind the scenes, news in the niche.

## Instructions
For each post, write one block:

- **Topic:** plain words.
- **Format:** the active format from formats.md this fits, with a one-line reason (or the one the owner picked). Skip if there is no formats file.
- **Goal:** one of follows, saves, shares, comments, DMs, clicks. Pick the one the call to action serves.
- **Emotion:** the one feeling the viewer leaves with (relief, curiosity, confidence, surprise, belonging, amusement). Choose from the brief's voice; if the voice is light, never pick fear or doom.
- **The one person:** who exactly is watching, in a sentence.
- **Angle:** the specific way in (the mistake, the shortcut, the before and after, the myth, the first step).
- **Call to action:** only from the brief's `offers` that are live. If the brief lists comment keywords, use one of those exactly; otherwise Follow or Save.
- **Claims check:** any claim this angle needs, and the `facts` line that supports it. No support means change the angle.

Balance the week: vary goals and emotions, and do not repeat an angle the brief's notes list as recent.

## Rules
- Never invent offers, prices, keywords or links.
- Anything on `never_say` is out, including near synonyms.
