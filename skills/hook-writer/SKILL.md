# Hook Writer

Three hooks per topic, scored, so the weak ones die before they cost a render. This scores hooks against a checklist. It does not predict views.

## When to use
After pick-angle. Also standalone to score a hook the owner already wrote. The factory runs it at step 5.

## What you'll need
The angle block (topic, goal, emotion, one person, call to action) and the brief's voice and never_say. Winners from the brief's notes, if any, show which hook types have worked for this business.

## Instructions
1. Write 3 variants per topic, each a different hook type (result first, mistake, question, contrast, demo, story). Each is the first spoken line plus the first on-screen text, which may differ.
2. Score each variant, 1 point per test:
   - **Complete claim in line one.** The first line makes sense alone; nothing waits for line two.
   - **Named payoff.** It says what the viewer gets (a result, a saved hour, the fix).
   - **Curiosity gap.** It leaves one specific thing unanswered that the video answers.
   - **Speaks to one person.** "You", one situation, not "everyone" or "people".
3. Anything under 4 of 4: say which test failed, rewrite it, and score again. Keep rewriting up to 3 times; if it still fails, replace it with a new variant.
4. If a format is chosen, its hook pattern and hook rule apply on top of the 4 tests. A retro hook and a news hook are judged inside their own lane.
5. Also check: speakable in about 2 seconds (roughly 12 words or fewer), in the brief's voice, nothing from never_say, no claim without a `facts` line.
6. Output per topic: the 3 final hooks with scores and a one-line reason, the recommended one first. The other 2 are test variants: the owner can post them as separate trial reels on different days and promote the winner by hand.

## Output format
```
Topic: ...
1. [4/4] Spoken: "..." | On screen: "..." | Why: ...
2. [4/4] ...
3. [4/4] ...
```

## Rules
- Say "scores your hooks" and never "predicts virality" or "guarantees views".
- No clickbait the video does not pay off. The payoff named in the hook must arrive in the script.
