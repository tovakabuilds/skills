# Study Format

Turn a post that worked into a format you can reuse with your own topic and voice.

## When to use
Before writing, to pick a structure that has already held attention in this niche. The factory runs it at step 2.

## What you'll need
One to three examples: links, video files, transcripts, screenshots or a plain description. If you can view the video, watch it; if not, work from what you are given and say so. If the owner gives nothing, ask for 2 posts they wish they had made.

## Instructions
For each example, fill this teardown:

1. **First second.** What moves, what is said or written, is the claim complete?
2. **Hook type.** Result first, mistake, question, contrast, list, story, demo, or other.
3. **Structure.** The beats in order with rough seconds (for example hook 0 to 2, problem 2 to 6, three steps 6 to 30, payoff, call to action).
4. **Visual rhythm.** How often the picture changes; does the picture match the word being spoken?
5. **On-screen text.** How much, where, how it animates.
6. **Call to action.** What it asks and how.
7. **Why it held attention.** One or two plain sentences.
8. **Reusable format.** A fill-in template using placeholders only, such as "[result] in [time] without [pain]".

Then give a short ranked list of the formats with one line each on fit for this business (from the brief: audience, voice, offers). Recommend one.

To save a format, follow the order in FORMATS-FILE.md (in this package):
1. Draft the whole format in the formats file shape.
2. Show it to the owner whole.
3. If the owner wants to keep it, save it to `formats.md` as `pending`.
4. It becomes `active` only on the owner's explicit confirmation.
5. A run that is waiting on it continues only after it is active.

## Rules
- Reuse the structure, write your own words. The template holds placeholders, not the creator's lines.
- Do not judge fit by the creator's style. Style comes from this business's brief.
- Note anything a format needs that the owner may not have (a face on camera, product footage, a demo).
